# The Installer is the only audience

A Notification goes to the person who installed the plugin, on phones subscribed to a Topic they control. A project cannot redirect Notifications to teammates, and a session cannot pick a different recipient.

Rejected: per-project recipients, and per-session routing. Those turn a pager into an address book. Topic, Broker, and token stay the Installer's, even when the plugin is enabled from a project config.
